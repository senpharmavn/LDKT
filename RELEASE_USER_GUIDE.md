# User Guide Release - Lanh Dao Khai Tam

Tai lieu nay dung de gioi thieu cach su dung ban release dau tien cua ung dung Lanh Dao Khai Tam cho 3 nhom: hoc vien, giang vien va admin van hanh.

## 1. Tong quan ung dung

Ung dung gom 2 phan:

- App hoc vien: dung tren dien thoai, uu tien trai nghiem nhanh, gon, de thao tac moi ngay.
- Dashboard giang vien: dung tren may tinh, theo doi hoc vien, workshop, mini challenge, nhom dong hanh va bao cao Leadership Assessment.

Du lieu hien tai dang ket noi voi Google Sheet thong qua Google Apps Script. Giai doan nay chua dung Firebase de giu toc do trien khai nhanh va de admin tu dieu chinh noi dung.

## 2. Huong dan cho hoc vien

### Dang nhap

1. Mo link app hoc vien.
2. Nhap email hoac so dien thoai da dang ky voi chuong trinh.
3. Nhap ma hoc vien duoc cung cap.
4. Bam Dang nhap.

Neu khong dang nhap duoc, hoc vien can lien he admin de kiem tra:

- Email/so dien thoai da dung voi danh sach hoc vien chua.
- Ma hoc vien co chinh xac khong.
- Trang thai hoc vien tren sheet co dang Active khong.

### Trang Home

Trang Home hien thi cac thong tin chinh:

- Workshop hien tai.
- Tien do ca nhan.
- XP.
- Streak dai nhat.
- Thu hang leaderboard.
- Mini Challenge dang mo neu co.
- Bang xep hang.

Hoc vien nen vao Home moi ngay de xem nhiem vu hien tai va check-in neu dang co Mini Challenge.

### Mini Challenge

Mini Challenge chi hien khi workshop tuong ung da mo va den ngay thuc hien challenge.

Trang thai ngay:

- Mau xanh: da check-in.
- Mau cam: ngay hien tai.
- Mau xam dam: ngay da qua nhung chua check-in.
- Mau trang/xam nhe: ngay chua toi.

Khi check-in, hoc vien ghi:

- Cam xuc hom nay.
- Dieu toi nhan ra hom nay.
- Mot hanh dong nho toi se thu ngay mai.

Noi dung nay duoc luu vao nhat ky insight ca nhan.

### Hanh trinh

Tab Hanh trinh cho hoc vien xem toan bo lo trinh chuong trinh:

- Workshop 0 den Workshop 7.
- Mini Challenge sau cac workshop neu co.
- Leadership Assessment.
- 21-days challenge.
- Share & Learn.

Workshop co trang thai done hoac now se co nut xem tai lieu. Workshop locked se chua mo.

### Danh gia Workshop

Khi admin mo form danh gia workshop, hoc vien se thay task trong tab Danh gia hoac Thong bao.

Hoc vien bam vao task de tra loi form danh gia workshop. Sau khi gui xong, task se khong hien lai.

### Leadership Assessment

Leadership Assessment gom 2 phan:

- Tu danh gia: hoc vien tu tra loi 30 tinh huong.
- Moi nguoi khac danh gia: hoc vien copy link va gui cho dong nghiep/ban be/cap tren danh gia.

Trong form tu danh gia, hoc vien nen chon phuong an sat voi suy nghi va cam xuc thuc te ben trong, dac biet khi gap ap luc, thay vi chon cach minh nghi la "nen lam".

Trong form moi nguoi khac danh gia, nguoi danh gia nen chon phuong an sat voi hanh vi thuc te ho nhin thay hoac trai nghiem o hoc vien, thay vi dua vao tinh cam ca nhan hay an tuong gan day.

### Bao cao Leadership Assessment

Sau khi hoc vien hoan thanh tu danh gia va nhan du danh gia tu nguoi khac, giang vien/admin se tao report tren dashboard.

Khi report duoc publish, hoc vien co the vao Ho so va bam mo Leadership Assessment de xem bao cao.

### Learning

Tab Learning dung de:

- Xem tai lieu bai hoc da mo.
- Xem flashcard/tai lieu recap neu workshop co link.
- Xem nhat ky insight ca nhan.
- Xem insight nhom trong giai doan 21-days challenge neu co nhom dong hanh.

### Nhom ban dong hanh

Nhom ban dong hanh chi nen mo trong giai doan 21-days challenge sau Workshop 7.

Hoc vien co the:

- Chon nhom.
- Xem thanh vien nhom.
- Check-in hop nhom theo tuan.
- Ghi insight va hanh dong tiep theo sau buoi hop nhom.

## 3. Huong dan cho giang vien

Dashboard giang vien dung tren laptop/desktop.

### Tong quan

Trang Tong quan giup giang vien xem nhanh:

- So hoc vien dang tham gia.
- Muc do tham gia.
- Ty le duy tri thuc hanh.
- Ty le duy tri danh gia.
- Hoc vien can chu y.
- Cac canh bao/cham tien do.

### Hoc vien

Trang Hoc vien giup xem danh sach hoc vien trong cohort:

- Ma hoc vien.
- Ho ten.
- Email.
- XP.
- Streak.
- So ngay check-in.
- So luot nhan danh gia.
- Trang thai tien do.

Giang vien co the bam vao tung hoc vien de xem chi tiet tien do va trang thai Leadership Assessment.

### Workshop Quality

Trang Workshop Quality dung de theo doi chat luong tung workshop:

- So nguoi tham gia workshop.
- Ty le tham gia danh gia.
- Diem trung binh.
- Chi so manh nhat.
- Chi so yeu nhat.
- Phan hoi van ban cua hoc vien.

4 cau hoi chung cua workshop gom:

- Ro rang.
- Lien quan.
- Facilitator.
- An toan chia se.

Du lieu nay lay tu cac cau hoi ALL1-ALL4 trong sheet Evaluation_Questions va Evaluation_Answers.

### Mini Challenge

Trang Mini Challenge dung de theo doi muc do thuc hanh:

- Ty le check-in tung challenge.
- Ty le hoan thanh tren 80%.
- Ty le hoan thanh duoi 80%.
- Ty le bo cuoc cho 21-days challenge.
- Cac hoc vien duy tri tot.
- Cac hoc vien can ho tro.

Ty le bo cuoc chi nen ap dung cho 21-days challenge.

### Nhom dong hanh

Trang nhom dong hanh giup giang vien xem:

- Danh sach nhom.
- Thanh vien tung nhom.
- Tuan nao nhom da check-in.
- Nhom nao chua hop hoac can ho tro.

Giang vien khong can xem chi tiet insight ca nhan cua hoc vien, chi can biet nhom da thuc hien check-in hay chua.

### Leadership Assessment Report

Khi hoc vien da co:

- 1 tu danh gia.
- Toi thieu 3 luot nguoi khac danh gia.

Dashboard se cho phep tao report.

Giang vien/admin bam Tao report de:

- Rebuild du lieu Assessment Summary.
- Tao link report.
- Luu link vao cot LeadershipReportLink trong sheet Users.
- Mo quyen xem report cho hoc vien.

## 4. Huong dan cho admin

Admin quan ly Google Sheet va Apps Script.

### Sheet Users

Cot nen co:

- UserID.
- FullName.
- Email.
- Phone.
- CompanyName neu can.
- EnrollmentDate.
- CohortID.
- Role.
- CurrentWorkshop.
- ParticipationStatus.
- Status.
- LeadershipReportLink.
- Token.
- XP.
- Cot diem danh WS0-WS7/S&L neu can.

Neu hoc vien dien form dang ky rieng, admin chi can copy cac thong tin can thiet qua Users, khong can dua toan bo cau tra loi dang ky vao sheet Users.

### Sheet Workshops

Dung de quan ly lo trinh:

- WorkshopID.
- Title.
- Description.
- Order.
- Status: done / now / locked.
- Flashcard URL hoac link tai lieu.
- Date.

Workshop co status done hoac now se mo tai lieu hoc tap.

### Sheet Mini_Challenges

Dung de quan ly cac ngay challenge.

Khong can tao Mini Challenge cho Workshop 0.

Neu xoa challenge trong sheet, app se khong hien challenge do o Home. Neu van hien o Hanh trinh, can kiem tra lai cache/deploy hoac du lieu fallback.

### Sheet Evaluation_Templates

Dung de mo/tat cac form:

- SELF-BEFORE: tu danh gia Leadership Assessment.
- OTHER-BEFORE: moi nguoi khac danh gia.
- WSx-MAIN: danh gia workshop.

Status:

- active: hien tren app.
- locked: an/khong cho thao tac.

### Sheet Evaluation_Questions

Dung de quan ly cau hoi:

- Cac cau hoi COMMON cho workshop.
- Cac cau hoi tinh huong cho Leadership Assessment.
- Cac cau hoi text cho nguoi khac danh gia.

Neu muon sua noi dung cau hoi, admin sua truc tiep trong sheet nay. App se doc noi dung moi sau khi du lieu duoc cap nhat.

### Sheet Evaluation_Responses va Evaluation_Answers

Day la raw data danh gia.

Khong nen sua tay neu khong can thiet.

### Sheet Student_Stats

Nen dung lam sheet tong hop nhe cho app hoc vien:

- XP.
- Rank.
- LongestStreak.
- CheckinCount.
- PeerFeedbackCount.
- SelfBeforeDone.
- LastCheckinAt.

App hoc vien doc sheet nay de nhanh hon, tranh phai tinh lai tu nhieu sheet raw.

### Sheet Assessment_Summary

Dung de tong hop du lieu Leadership Assessment sau khi giang vien/admin tao report.

Trang thai report:

- data_ready / ready: du dieu kien tao report.
- published: report da duoc tao va hoc vien co the xem.

## 5. Quy trinh van hanh de release

### Truoc khi bat dau cohort

1. Admin cap nhat Users.
2. Cap nhat Workshops.
3. Cap nhat Mini_Challenges.
4. Kiem tra Evaluation_Templates.
5. Kiem tra Evaluation_Questions.
6. Chay lai Apps Script neu co thay doi code.
7. Kiem tra link app hoc vien va dashboard giang vien.

### Trong qua trinh hoc

1. Mo workshop theo tung giai doan bang status now/done/locked.
2. Mo form danh gia workshop khi can.
3. Theo doi check-in va XP tren dashboard.
4. Ho tro hoc vien khong dang nhap duoc hoac chua thay task.

### Sau phan Leadership Assessment

1. Kiem tra hoc vien da hoan thanh tu danh gia.
2. Kiem tra so luot nguoi khac danh gia.
3. Khi da du du lieu, giang vien/admin tao report.
4. Kiem tra link report duoc ghi vao Users.LeadershipReportLink.
5. Hoc vien vao Ho so de xem report.

## 6. Avatar hoc vien - co nen cho upload anh khong?

### Tac dong neu cho upload anh

Neu hoc vien upload anh truc tiep vao app, se phat sinh cac van de:

- Can noi luu file anh: Google Drive, Firebase Storage, Cloudinary hoac server rieng.
- Google Sheet khong phu hop de luu file anh, chi nen luu URL anh.
- Anh ca nhan la du lieu nhay cam hon ten/email, can can nhac quyen rieng tu.
- Upload anh co the lam app cham hon neu anh nang hoac khong duoc toi uu.
- Can them logic nen anh, resize anh, validate file, fallback khi anh loi.

### Phuong an nhe neu van muon co avatar

Giai doan hien tai nen dung 1 trong 2 cach:

1. Giu avatar chu cai viet tat nhu hien tai.
2. Admin them cot AvatarURL trong sheet Users, neu co URL anh thi app hien anh, neu khong co thi hien chu cai viet tat.

Phuong an 2 khong anh huong nhieu den app hoc vien neu:

- Anh da duoc resize nho truoc.
- Dung URL cong khai hoac URL co quyen xem phu hop.
- App co fallback ve chu cai neu anh khong load duoc.

### Khuyen nghi

Chua nen cho hoc vien tu upload avatar trong giai doan Google Sheet.

Neu can lam dep giao dien, nen them cot AvatarURL de admin gan anh sau. Khi chuyen sang Firebase, co the lam tinh nang upload avatar dung chuan hon.

## 7. Luu y gioi han hien tai

- Google Sheet phu hop voi MVP va cohort nho.
- Khi so hoc vien tang, nen dung Student_Stats va cac sheet summary de app hoc vien chay nhanh.
- Dashboard giang vien co the cham hon app hoc vien vi can doc nhieu sheet hon, nhung chap nhan duoc neu dung de quan sat va bao cao.
- Neu sau nay can bao mat cao hon va lam app mobile native, nen chuyen sang Firebase hoac backend rieng.
